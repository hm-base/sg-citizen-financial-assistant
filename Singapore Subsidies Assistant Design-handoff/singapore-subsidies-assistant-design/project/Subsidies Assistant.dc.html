Yes, in most cases. Cash top-ups you make to your mother's CPF Retirement Account can be claimed under CPF Cash Top-up Relief, up to $8,000 per calendar year for top-ups to loved ones.[1]

The relief goes to the person who made the top-up, in the Year of Assessment after the year you paid it. Your mother must be a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident.[2] If she is 55 or older, the source states the top-up goes to her Retirement Account, up to the Full Retirement Sum.[3]

This answer summarises published documents. It is not tax advice, and not confirmation that a claim will be allowed. Check the cited sections before you file.
