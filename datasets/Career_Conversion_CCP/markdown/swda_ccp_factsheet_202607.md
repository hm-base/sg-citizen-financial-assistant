FACTSHEET ON SWDA’S CAREER CONVERSION PROGRAMME 
Skills and Workforce Development’s Career Conversion Programmes (CCPs) help 
employers broaden their talent pool by reskilling mid -career new hires or existing 
employees into growth job roles with good longer -term prospects with salary support of 
up to 90% .  
 
1) CCP Modes: 
 
a) Place-and-Train: Company hires jobseeker and puts the individual through on -
the-job training (OJT) to take on a new growth job role. 
b) Attach-and-Train: Company acts as host employer to provide jobseeker with 
training and work attachments, in growth jobs roles where there are good  
prospects for placement. 
c) Job Redesign (JR) Reskilling: Company sends an existing employee for reskilling, 
to take on growth job roles aligned to the Jobs Transformation Maps (JTMs). 
 
Growth job roles are emerging or good job opportunities with long-term prospects for 
progression, identified in each sector  based on industry transformation trends and 
future workforce needs. 
The types of training that mid -career individuals would undergo through the CCPs are 
broadly summarised in Table 1 below.

2) Coverage of CCPs 
SWDA currently offers CCPs in around 30 sectors to help mid -career jobseekers or 
existing employees reskill and take on new job roles with good prospects and

The CCPs support reskilling in growth job roles aligned with the sectoral development

Since April 2025, CCPs have been enhanced to support the reskilling of mid-career new

3) Benefits of CCPs 
 
• Facilitate recruitment of career switchers to meet employers’ manpower needs 
• Help mid-career individuals to reskill and take on new job roles 
• Leverage structured industry-recognised training 
• Reskill existing employees to take on new growth job roles aligned with JTMs  
• Tap on Government funding through Salary Support / Training Allowance

Interested mid-career individuals and companies will need to meet the following criteria:

Mid-career individuals: 
• Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident 
• Minimum 21 years old 
• Graduated, or completed National Service, for at least 2 years 3 
• Career conversion is required --  
a) New Hire: New job role trained for under the CCP should be substantially 
different from previous job role(s)4, 
b) Existing Employee: New job role should be growth job roles identified under 
the JTMs 
Beyond the general eligibility criteria, different CCPs may have additional course-specific 
entry requirements, and employers may apply their own shortlisting process.  
In general, individuals applying for CCPs should also not be: 
• Shareholders of the CCP participating company, or its related entities 
• Related to individuals in the CCP participating company who have the authority to 
make decisions in relation to the CCP application 
• Immediate ex-staff of the CCP participating company or its related entities 
• Related to the owners of the CCP participating company  
 Please refer to the FAQs for more details on these exclusions.

• Must be able to offer an employment contract on permanent terms or on

which the CCP is for, with remuneration that is commensurate with the market 
rate

6) How to Apply? 
Interested individuals can apply for CCP-supportable job opportunities on 
MyCareersFuture, where job postings will indicate the relevant CCP. Interested 
employers can explore the various CCPs available and register for them at the SWDA 
website.
