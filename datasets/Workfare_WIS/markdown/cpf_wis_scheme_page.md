Overview 

Helping you save for the future 

CPF payments that grow your savings for retirement, housing and healthcare, plus cash payments to supplement your income, are some of the support that you can tap on.

**About Workfare**

 

Introduced in 2007, WIS supplements the incomes and CPF savings of lower-income workers to help them save for retirement.

Workfare is targeted at Singaporean workers whose earnings are in the bottom 20%, with some support for those slightly above.

 Over S$12.9 billion1 of WIS has been disbursed to 1.13 million1 lower-income workers.

 

1 _As at February 2026, up to WY2025 2 WIS_

 

2 _Work Year (WY) refers to the calendar year of work done._

[ View more ](</member/growing-your-savings/government-support/workfare-income-supplement/about-workfare>)

Workfare is paid in the form of CPF payments and cash to supplement their incomes.

* * *

Employee 

WIS for employees 

If you are an eligible employee, you will receive CPF payments to help you save for retirement, plus cash payments to supplement your income.

Eligibility

You will qualify for WIS if you:

  * are a Singapore Citizen;
  * are at least 30 years old or older as of 31 December of the work year, or persons with disabilities of any age;
  * earn a gross monthly income1 of at least $5002, and not more than $3,000 (previously $2,500 for Work Year 2024) for the month worked as an employee;
  * earn an average gross monthly income of not more than $3,000 in the past 12 months3 (previously $2,500 for Work Year 2024) as an employee;
  * live in a property with an annual value4 of $21,000 and below from Work Year 2024, as at 31 December of the preceding year;
  * own not more than one property5; and
  * if you are married, you and your spouse together not own more than one property and the assessable income of your spouse does not exceed $70,000 of the preceding Year of Assessment.

 

1 _Gross monthly income includes basic salary and extra wages such as overtime pay, commissions and bonuses._

 

2 _  Persons with disabilities, workers who qualify for ComCare Short-to-Medium-Term Assistance and caregivers of care recipients (generally include caregivers residing with care recipients who are medically certified to have permanent moderate to severe disabilities) will qualify for concessionary WIS, even if they earn less than $500.   
Please click [here](<https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/can-i-qualify-for-workfare-income-supplement-if-i-earn-less-than-500-per-month>) for more information on the eligibility criteria for concessionary WIS._

 

3 _Defined as sum of income earned in the past 12-month period ÷ Total number of months worked in the past 12-month period_

 

4 _For more information on the computation of annual value, please visit the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) website._

 

5 _Includes all types of properties, e.g. Housing & Development Board flat, private property and non-residential property._

* * *

Receiving WIS payments

How much you can receive

The table below shows the maximum WIS amount you can receive each year, based on your age group.

**Age** | **Maximum WIS per year**  
---|---  
**For Work Year 2024** | **For Work Year 2025**  
30-34 | $2,100 | $2,450  
35-44 | $3,000 | $3,500  
45-59 | $3,600 | $4,200  
60 and above | $4,200 | $4,900  
Persons with disabilities will receive the same amount as those who are aged 60 and above.  
  
You can also use the online WIS calculator to estimate how much you will receive.

[ Calculate your WIS ](<https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/calculators#section-header-379892678>)

* * *

How WIS payments are allocated

You will receive 40% of your WIS in cash and 60% in CPF contributions. We will credit the cash portion to your [PayNow NRIC-linked bank account](<https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/what-is-paynow-and-how-do-i-link-my-nric-to-paynow>) if you have one. Otherwise, it will be credited to the bank account that you have registered to receive Government benefits, or via [GovCash](<https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/what-is-govcash>) if you had not registered a bank account with us. If you need to update your local bank account details, you can do so after logging in to the e-Services.

[ Update bank details ](<https://www.govpayouts.gov.sg/cds/main/login>)

* * *

When you will receive your WIS payments

As an employee, you will receive WIS every month. The table below shows when you will receive the payments.  

**If you worked in** | **You will receive the WIS in your bank account or via GovCash**  
---|---  
**PayNow-NRIC / Bank Crediting** | **GovCash**  
January | End March | 1st week of April  
February | End April | 1st week of May  
March | End May | 1st week of June  
Month x | End of month (x+2) | 1st week of the following month (x+3)  
  
[ Check WIS payments ](<https://www.govpayouts.gov.sg/cds/main/login>)

* * *

Actions required

If you are an employee, there’s no further action required

Your eligibility for WIS will be automatically assessed based on the income declared by your employer(s) for work done in that month, if you/your employer has made CPF contributions. 

If your employer is not contributing CPF on your behalf, please approach your employer to clarify on the discrepancies. If your employer does not provide you with a satisfactory response, you can [lodge a report](<https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/how-can-i-lodge-a-report-for-non-payment-or-underpayment-of-cpf-contributions>) with CPF Board.

* * *

If you worked as both an employee and a self-employed person,

You will first be assessed for your work done as an employee and you will receive monthly WIS if you meet the criteria.

 

After the work year, we will assess your eligibility for additional WIS, based on your total income for the year (both employee and self-employed income).

* * *

Self-employed 

WIS for self-employed persons 

If you are an eligible self-employed person (SEP), you can get MediSave payments to help you save for your healthcare needs, plus cash payments to supplement your income.

Eligibility

You will qualify for WIS if you:

  * are a Singapore Citizen;
  * are at least 30 years old or older as of 31 December of the work year, or persons with disabilities of any age;
  * earn an average monthly income of at least $5001, and not more than $3,000 (previously $2,500 for Work Year 2024);
  * have declared your net trade income and made MediSave contributions in full;
  * live in a property with an annual value2 of $21,000 and below from Work Year 2024, assessed as at 31 December of the preceding year;
  * own not more than one property3; and
  * if you are married, you and your spouse together own not more than one property and the assessable income of your spouse does not exceed $70,000 for the preceding Year of Assessment.

1 _  Persons with disabilities, workers who qualify for ComCare Short-to-Medium-Term Assistance and caregivers of care recipients (generally include caregivers residing with care recipients who are medically certified to have permanent moderate to severe disabilities) will qualify for concessionary WIS, even if they earn less than $500. Please click [here](<https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/can-i-qualify-for-workfare-income-supplement-if-i-earn-less-than-500-per-month>) for more information on the eligibility criteria for concessionary WIS._

When you will receive your WIS payments

If you are a self-employed person (SEP), you will get WIS once a year for work done in the previous Work Year (WY).1

 

The earliest you can get it is by end April of the following WY (e.g. by end April 2026 for Work Year 2025), if the following are completed by 31 March:

  * You have declared your income to IRAS2, and CPF Board has received your assessed Net Trade Income (NTI)
  * You have made your MediSave contributions in full based on your declared NTI earned in the WY, _without_ accounting for (i) previous years’ losses and (ii) net earnings from platform work, if applicable.

If you declare your income and make your full MediSave contributions after 31 March, you will get your WIS within two months after you complete making your MediSave contributions. Hence, the earlier you declare your income and make the required MediSave contribution, the sooner you will receive your WIS.

 

1 _Eligible SEPs get WIS yearly because their NTI for the entire year can only be confirmed in the following year. Employees and platform workers get WIS monthly because their income is automatically reported through monthly CPF contributions._

 

2 _  If you receive a notification to file tax from IRAS, or you meet the requirement to file tax (i.e. NTI above $6,000 and/or Total Income above $22,000), please submit your tax return to IRAS directly from 1 March to 18 April of the following WY. If you do not receive a notification from IRAS or do not meet the requirement to file tax, please e-File your tax return to IRAS via myTax Portal, from 1 March to 31 October of the following WY._

Actions required

**1\. You will need to declare your income**

 

**_For Work Year 2023 (WY2023) onwards_**

All Net Trade Income (NTI)1 declarations from Work Year 2023 (Year of Assessment 2024) onwards can be made with the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) by filing a tax return. There is no need to file separate declarations with CPF Board.

 

If you receive a notification (via letter/form/SMS) to file tax from IRAS, or you meet the requirement to file tax (i.e. NTI above $6,000 and/or Total Income above $22,000), please complete and submit the tax return to IRAS directly. The income tax filing period is from 1 March to 18 April annually.

 

If you do not receive a notification from IRAS or do not meet the requirement to file tax (i.e. NTI is less than or equal to $6,000 and/or Total Income less than or equal to $22,000), please declare your self-employed NTI to IRAS via [myTax Portal __](<https://mytax.iras.gov.sg/login/selection>) so that your eligibility for WIS can be assessed. The portal will be available from 1 March to 31 October annually for taxpayers to e-File tax returns. 

 

Once IRAS has assessed your income for the year, your assessed NTI will be transmitted to the Board. You can check your updated NTI records by logging in to the Self-employment dashboard with your Singpass.

 

1 NTI is your gross trade income minus all allowable business expenses, capital allowances and trade losses as determined by IRAS.

This amount may differ from the MediSave contributions you are required to fulfil as a SEP under the [Self-Employed Scheme (SES)](<https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/growing-your-savings/cpf-contributions/saving-as-a-self-employed-person>) which may be adjusted based on previous years' losses or excess MediSave contributions.

You may refer to any of the following sources for the amount of MediSave contribution that you need to make to qualify for WIS:

 

i) WIS e-services: Login to the WIS [e-services __](<https://www.govpayouts.gov.sg/cds/main/login>)with your Singpass to check your MediSave liability payable.

 

ii) WIS calculator:  Estimate the amount of MediSave payable using the [WIS calculator](<https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/calculators#section-header-379892678>) for SEPs.

Platform Worker 

WIS for platform workers 

From 2025, eligible platform workers will receive WIS monthly instead of annually, as platform operators will deduct CPF contributions from platform workers as and when they earn and submit them to CPF Board every month.

 

From 2029, eligible platform workers who are mandated or opt in to the increased CPF contributions will receive higher WIS payments, at the same level as employees, when their CPF contribution rates fully align with that of employees. Eligible platform workers who have not opted in to the increased CPF contributions will continue to receive WIS at the same level as SEPs.

 

[Find out more about CPF contributions for platform workers](<https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/growing-your-savings/cpf-contributions/saving-as-a-platform-worker>)

Eligibility

You will qualify for WIS if you:

  * are a Singapore Citizen;
  * are at least 30 years old or older as of 31 December of the work year, or persons with disabilities of any age;
  * earn monthly income (including net earnings from platform work) of at least $5001, and not more than $3,000 (from Work Year 2025 onwards) for the month worked;
  * earn an average gross monthly income (including net earnings from platform work) of not more than $3,000 (from Work Year 2025 onwards) in the past 12 months
  * live in a property with an annual value2 of $21,000 and below from Work Year 2024, as at 31 December of the preceding year;
  * own not more than one property3; and
  * if you are married, you and your spouse together not own more than one property and the assessable income of your spouse does not exceed $70,000 of the preceding Year of Assessment.

1 _Persons with disabilities, workers who qualify for ComCare Short-to-Medium-Term Assistance and caregivers of care recipients (generally include caregivers residing with care recipients who are medically certified to have permanent moderate to severe disabilities) will qualify for concessionary WIS, even if they earn less than $500.  _

_Please click[here](<https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/can-i-qualify-for-workfare-income-supplement-if-i-earn-less-than-500-per-month>) for more information on the eligibility criteria for concessionary WIS._

 

2 _For more information on the computation of annual value, please visit the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) website._

 

_3  Includes all types of properties, e.g. Housing & Development Board flat, private property and non-residential property._
