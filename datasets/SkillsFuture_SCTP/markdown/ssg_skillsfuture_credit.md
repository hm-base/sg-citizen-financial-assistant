# SkillsFuture Credit

### What is it?

SkillsFuture Credit helps Singaporeans offset course fees for eligible courses that support skills development and lifelong learning to be future-ready and relevant

### Who is it for?

All Singapore Citizens aged 25 and above

### What can I use it for?

Courses listed on MySkillsFuture portal (credit eligibility varies by course)

**Did you know?**  
---  
You can use your SkillsFuture Credit (SFC) to sign up for online subscriptions, such as Coursera, Udemy and more. Find out more [here](<https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/education-career-personal-development/use_SFC_for_online_subscriptions_and_courses.html>)!  
  
## SkillsFuture Credit at a Glance 

There are two tiers of SkillsFuture Credit:  
1\. Base Tier (for Singapore Citizens aged 25 and above), and  
2\. Mid-Career Tier (for Singapore Citizens aged 40 and above).

Type  | Eligibility  | Credit Amount | Expiry  
---|---|---|---  
Base Tier: Opening SkillsFuture Credit |   Singapore Citizens aged 25 and above   | $500 Can be used for **all courses** on MySkillsFuture portal |   Does not expire    
Mid-Career Tier: SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career) |   Singapore Citizens aged 40 and above   | $4,000 Can be used for **selected courses** on MySkillsFuture portal |   Does not expire    
  
## Types of SkillsFuture Credit 

###  

### 1\. Opening SkillsFuture Credit — $500

This base tier credit can be used on top of existing SkillsFuture course fee subsidies to pay for a wide range of approved skills-related courses.

It has no expiry date and is provided to all Singapore Citizens aged 25 and above.  

All courses listed on MySkillsFuture portal are eligible for SkillsFuture Credit (Opening Credit). They include:

  1. Courses subsidised or approved by the Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA), 
  2. Selected courses offered by Ministry of Education-funded institutions such as Institute of Technical Education, polytechnics, autonomous universities, LASALLE College of the Arts and Nanyang Academy of Fine Arts,
  3. Courses supported by lead agencies, and 
  4. SkillsFuture@PA courses offered by the People’s Association and selected courses under the National Silver Academy. 

###  

### 2. **SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career)  — $4,000**

This credit supports Singapore Citizens aged 40 and above. It can be used to offset out-of-pocket course fees for [**selected courses**](<https://courses.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/search?page=1&Tags_text_SFInitiatives=SkillsFuture%20Credit%20\(Mid-Career\)>) that have better employability outcomes, and it does not expire.

Eligible courses include:

  1. Institute of Higher Learning (IHL) full qualifications and shorter modules that stack to a full qualification,
  2. MOE-subsidised full qualifications from the University of the Arts Singapore and Arts Institutions,
  3. [SkillsFuture Career Transition Programmes](<https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/education-career-personal-development/skillsfuture-career-transition-programme.html>) (SCTP), which are train-and-place programmes in sectors with good hiring opportunities,
  4. Courses aligned to training requirements in Progressive Wage Model (PWM) sectors, and
  5. Workplace Literacy and Numeracy courses.

**Note** : Any balance from the one-off **Additional SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career Support)** , which was provided to every Singapore Citizen aged 40 to 60 (inclusive) as at 31 December 2020, is now merged with the **SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career)** and no longer carries an expiry date.

## How to Start Using Your SkillsFuture Credit 

  1. Log in with Singpass to check your available credit balance. Here’s [how](<https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/how-to-guides/check-sfc-balance.html>).
  2. Browse eligible courses on the MySkillsFuture portal. Here’s [how](<https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/how-to-guides/myskillsfuture-course-search-guide.html>).
  3. Submit your claim online before the course start date. Learn about key timelines, the supporting documents you need, and how to submit the claim _[here](<https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/how-to-guides/claim-sfc.html>)_.

For updates on SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career) top-up and other schemes under the [SkillsFuture Level-Up Programme](<http://myskillsfuture.gov.sg/level-up>), subscribe to our [mailing list](<https://go.gov.sg/level-up-interest>).

## Top FAQs For SkillsFuture Credit 

**What can I use my SkillsFuture Credit for?**

You can use your SkillsFuture Credit (Base Tier) for all courses listed on MySkillsFuture portal.

SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career) can only be used for selected courses. The course directory and detail page for these courses will display the relevant label.

SkillsFuture Credit can be used on top of other government course subsidies.

**How do I submit a claim for SkillsFuture Credit?**

Please refer to the guide [here](<https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/portal/en/career-resources/career-resources/how-to-guides/claim-sfc.html>).

**Can I use my   SkillsFuture Credit (Base Tier) for a course that is not listed on MySkillsFuture portal? How can I do so?**

If you have identified a good course that supports your employment but it is not found on MySkillsFuture portal, you could send your request to us via our [service portal](<https://service-portal.swda.gov.sg/>). We will evaluate your request and inform you of the outcome within 10 working days.

We will only review requests to use SkillsFuture Credit (Base Tier) for course(s) completed not more than 6 months from the date of receipt of request.

**Can I use my  SkillsFuture (Mid-Career) for a course that is not listed on MySkillsFuture portal?**

You can only use your SkillsFuture Credit (Mid-Career) for courses that fall into the following categories:

1.       Institutes of Higher Learning (IHL) full qualifications and shorter modules that stack to a full qualification,

2.       MOE-subsidised full qualifications from the University of the Arts Singapore and Arts Institutions,

3.       SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme (SCTP), which are train-and-place programmes in sectors with good hiring opportunities,

4.       Courses aligned to training requirements in Progressive Wage Model (PWM) sectors, and 

5.       Workplace Literacy and Numeracy courses.
