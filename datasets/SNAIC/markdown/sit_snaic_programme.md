SNAIC AI Programme

Accelerate Your Future with Hands-On AI Training Designed to Upskill, Innovate and Transform Industries

## Become an Industry-Ready AI Practitioner in 6 Months

The **Singapore Institute of Technology x NVIDIA AI Centre (SNAIC)** is a pioneering collaboration between SIT and NVIDIA, created to accelerate AI adoption across industries and nurture Singapore’s next generation of AI talent.

As part of the National AI Strategy 2.0, SNAIC bridges academia and industry through applied research, innovation, and education. [Learn more about SNAIC >](<https://www.singaporetech.edu.sg/sit-x-nvidia-ai-centre-snaic>) 

The **SNAIC AI Programme** , developed in collaboration with the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA), is a full-time, six-month programme designed to train fresh graduates and mid-career professionals to become AI professionals.

Supported under the TechSkills Accelerator (TeSA)* initiative, the programme offers:

  * **Two months** **of intensive AI training** based on modules developed by SNAIC, equipping participants with the latest AI skills and tools such as **Generative AI, Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmentation Generation, and Agentic AI.**  
 
  * **Four months** **of hands-on projects** with industry partners, under the supervision of instructors and experts from SNAIC, enabling participants to **solve real-world business challenges using AI**.  
 
  * **Monthly stipend of S$4,000** for participants over the 6-month duration of the programme.

 

Note:  
* TechSkills Accelerator (TeSA) is a national initiative driven by Singapore’s Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA). Its goal is to build and develop a skilled ICT workforce to support Singapore’s digital economy. For more information, visit IMDA's website [here](<https://www.imda.gov.sg/how-we-can-help/techskills-accelerator-tesa>).

 

## Why Join SNAIC AI Programme?

#####    
Powerhouse Collaboration**: SIT x NVIDIA**

Gain exclusive access to the combined strengths of **Singapore’s university for industry** and**the world’s leading AI company**. 

This first-of-its-kind collaboration delivers unmatched academic rigor, industry relevance, and exposure to global AI innovations.

#####    
**Applied, Future-Ready Curriculum**

Master cutting-edge AI technologies like**Generative AI** , **LLMs** , **RAG** , **Agentic AI** , and**AI-assisted code generation** through hands-on labs, hackathons, and real-world projects.

Benefit from curriculum designed to meet the demands of today’s AI-driven industries.

##### ****  
**Real Industry Projects & Mentorship**

Work on live AI challenges from top companies across sectors such as healthcare, transport, and manufacturing. 

Be mentored by **SNAIC instructors** ,**NVIDIA experts** , and**industry partners** , ensuring practical impact and career readiness.

##### ****  
**Certifications & Career Pathways**

Earn**NVIDIA DLI certifications** , a **SIT Certificate of Attainment** , and potentially gain **academic credit recognition** toward SIT degrees.

Graduates are equipped to transition into AI roles or pursue further academic advancement.

 

## Who Is This Programme For?

Eligible for both **Singaporean and PR** with:

  * **Ability to commit** to a six-month, full-time schedule, including daily in-person sessions and a team-based industry project.
  * **Basic Python programming knowledge** , including variables, control structures, functions, and data handling.
  * **Recognised** **degree** ; or **diploma in a relevant discipline**  with a minimum of **two years of relevant work experience**.
