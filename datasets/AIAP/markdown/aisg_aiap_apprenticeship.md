## Build What Matters   
as an AI Engineer

Becoming a successful AI Engineer isn’t just about understanding theory — it’s about delivering AI solutions that solve real-world challenges.  
  
Through AIAP, you’ll make that shift from learner to builder.  
Gain hands-on experience writing production-grade code, optimising cloud infrastructure, and solving the complex challenges that keep intelligent systems secure, scalable, and reliable.  
  
This isn’t abstract learning. It’s practical, high-impact work — the kind that top companies are hiring for. With strong career prospects and competitive compensation, your path into AI has never been more accessible.  
  
Your AI Engineering journey starts here with AIAP.

## What to Expect throughout your AIAP Journey?

AIAP is structured with two phases, taking you from deep-skilling  
in key AI domains to real-world project deployment.

## Phase 1:  
Deep-Skilling (3 Months)

Structured training covering end-to-end  
AI Engineering — from classical ML models to Large Language Models, MLOps, Computer Vision, and AI Governance.

## Phase 2:  
Project Phase (3 or 6 Months)

Work in teams on real-world AI projects from the industry, alongside full-time, experienced AI Engineers, MLOps Engineers, Project Managers, and Principal Investigators.

## Your Path into   
AI Engineering Starts Here   
– For Those Who Qualify

AIAP is built for those who are serious about breaking into AI Engineering; whether you’re a fresh graduate ﬁnding your footing, or a mid-career professional ready to pivot to AI.  
  
It doesn’t matter if you come from a technical or non-technical background — AIAP is designed to equip you with everything you need to transition into industry-ready AI roles.

## This apprenticeship programme is open exclusively to:

Singapore Citizens

Holders of a NITEC, Diploma, or Degree from a recognised Institute of Higher Learning

Those eligible for TeSA CLT Funding

## What It Takes to Get In

Here’s everything you need to know about applying for AIAP.

## Knowledge & Proficiency Requirements*

## Programming

Writes clean, maintainable code following industry software engineering practices.

## Machine Learning

Experience with EDA and building end-to-end machine learning pipeline.

## MLOps & Deployment

Proﬁcient in Git/GitHub, Docker, Linux shell scripting, and at least one major cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or GCP).

## Data Technologies

Familiar with databases (SQL/NoSQL) and data processing tools like Apache Spark or Hadoop.

## Documentation

Able to produce basic code documentation such as README ﬁles and docstrings.

*Want to prepare for the technical requirements for AIAP?  
Consider the [AIAP Foundation course](<http://aiap.sg/foundation>) to build readiness for your application.

## Time Commitment

AIAP is a full-time programme. You will be expected to:

**Deep-skill and work on**  
**real-world AI projects in-person at the NTU campus.**

**Commit to the full duration of 6 or 9 months, depending on your assigned track.**

## Selection Process

All applicants must complete the two-stage AIAP Proﬁciency Test  
to assess technical readiness.

## Stage 1: Technical Assessment

A 6-day take-home covering EDA and ML in Python, built with software engineering rigour.

## Stage 2: Interview

A technical interview followed by a collaborative group case study.

Successful applicants are matched to either the 6- or 9-month track  
based on their results and profile.

## FAQs

###### **What is the AI Apprenticeship Programme (AIAP)?**

AIAP is a structured 6- or 9-month AI Engineering apprenticeship designed to build production-ready capability. Apprentices work on real-world projects, receive mentorship and undergo focused deep-skilling aligned with industry needs. The programme prepares participants for roles such as AI Engineer, MLOps Engineer, DataOps Engineer, and Data Scientist.

###### **What is the difference between the 6-month and 9-month tracks?**

The programme runs for either 6 or 9 months, depending on your background and assessment outcome. The 9-month AIAP is the flagship track with a proven track record of developing industry-ready AI Engineers.

Our intensive 6-month track, AIAP for Industry, is designed to accelerate AI Engineering deep-skilling while maintaining the same rigorous standards of training and mentorship.

Both tracks uphold the same quality and outcome expectations, enabling apprentices to build real-world capability at pace.

###### **Is the programme full-time and where is it conducted?**

Yes. AIAP is a full-time 6 or 9 months programme.

The programme is conducted on the NTU campus, where apprentices undergo structured AI Engineering training, mentorship and project work in an immersive learning environment.

###### **What kind of projects will I get to work on during AIAP?**

Apprentices work on real-world AI projects in collaboration with industry partners. Projects cover a range of practical challenges, ranging from Natural Language Processing for speech synthesis, to object detection for brand compliance, and other AI solutions that solve industry problems. This hands-on experience prepares you to design, build, and deploy production-ready AI systems.

###### **Do I need prior AI or coding experience?**

You do not need a formal background or past work experience in tech, but you do need foundational knowledge in Python programming, machine learning, and software engineering to pass the technical assessment. AIAP is an intensive deep-skilling program, not a beginner’s coding bootcamp.

###### **How can I best prepare for my AIAP application?**

Set yourself up for success before you apply.  
  
Explore our online resources, including the [AIAP Field Guide](<https://aiap.sg/aiap-field-guide/>) — a structured learning resource that helps aspiring, experienced, and returning applicants assess and build their AI skills as a head start before joining AIAP.  
  
To get a feel for the selection process, work through the [AIAP Technical Assessment Past Years Series](<https://aiap.sg/aiap-technical-assessment-past-years-series/>) and benchmark your readiness against real assessment questions used in previous batches.  
  
Not quite there yet? That’s what the [AIAP Foundation](<https://aiap.sg/foundation/>) course is for.  
The AIAP Foundation course is for those with basic Python skills, offering a fully guided, self-paced learning experience to master core AI fundamentals and applied AI skills, helping you build readiness for your AIAP application.  
  
Whether you’re switching careers or levelling up your technical skills, the AIAP Foundation gives you a structured, confidence-building pathway to prepare you for applying to AIAP.

###### ****What roles do apprentices typically secure after completion?****

Beyond becoming an AI Engineer, the deep-skilling and hands-on experience gained through AIAP open doors to a range of AI careers. Our graduates have secured roles such as AI Engineer, MLOps Engineer, DataOps Engineer, and Data Scientist. Since its inception, AIAP has maintained more than 90% placement rate, with graduates successfully transitioning into AI-related roles after programme completion.

###### ****Is AIAP recognised by employers?****

Yes. AIAP is an award winning programme to develop Singaporean AI talents and enhance career opportunities in AI-related roles. The programme is supported at the national level and recognised by leading employers across industries. Graduates have been placed with reputable organisations including Dyson, Tiktok, HP, and Bank of Singapore to name a few.

###### ****What is the application process?****

Once the application window opens, interested candidates can apply directly through the official AIAP website. Applicants should prepare their resume, academic transcripts and any relevant project experience before applying.

Shortlisted candidates will undergo assessments and interviews to evaluate technical proficiency, problem-solving ability and programme fit. Successful applicants will receive an offer for the appropriate track.

###### ****What if I do not qualify for AIAP?****

Not qualifying doesn’t mean the door is closed — it just means there’s more ground to cover first.

If you’re not quite ready for AIAP, the [AIAP Foundation](<https://aiap.sg/foundation/>) course is the perfect place to start. Designed for those with basic Python skills, it offers a fully guided, self-paced learning experience to help you build core AI fundamentals and applied AI skills — giving you a structured pathway to work towards your AIAP application.

You can also use the [AIAP Field Guide](<https://aiap.sg/aiap-field-guide/>) to assess where you currently stand and identify the skills you need to develop, and work through the [AIAP Technical Assessment Past Years Series](<https://aiap.sg/aiap-technical-assessment-past-years-series/>) to benchmark your readiness at your own pace.

Take the time to build your skills, and apply again when you’re ready. Many of our successful apprentices didn’t get in on their first attempt.
